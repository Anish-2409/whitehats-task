### A recent cybersecurity incident I read about
**The Lenovo & Dropbox Account Takeover Flaw (September 2026)**
I recently read an article on BleepingComputer about how attackers found a logic bug in Lenovo's email verification system. Basically, the flaw let hackers create fake Lenovo IDs using other people's email addresses without actually verifying them. 

The crazy part was the chain reaction: because Dropbox allowed users to click "Sign in with Lenovo," the attackers used those unverified Lenovo accounts to log straight into around 5,000 matching Dropbox accounts without needing a password. It stood out to me because nobody had to crack encryption or write complex malware—it was just a simple verification oversight on one site that ended up compromising user files on a completely different platform.
